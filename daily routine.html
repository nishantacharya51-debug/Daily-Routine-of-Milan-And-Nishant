Best practice for guaranteed alarms

Use Windows Clock alarms and allow Chrome notifications in Windows. The deployment section below includes exact steps.

Browser limitations to know

Browsers restrict autoplaying audio. Your first click on “Enable loud sound” unlocks audio for later beeps. Notification permission should be requested via a user gesture. Notifications require HTTPS in supporting browsers.

Safety note

“Loud” is limited by your device volume. Avoid uncomfortable sound levels.

Privacy and analytics

No third-party analytics by default. This template stores your data locally and does not send it anywhere.

Data is stored using your browser’s local storage and stays across sessions on the same device/browser profile. You can export/import or delete everything at any time.

If you add analytics later

If you introduce analytics cookies or similar tracking, you may need user consent depending on jurisdiction. Prefer first-party, privacy-preserving approaches and collect the minimum necessary.
